# Phone release check

Use the Vercel preview over HTTPS on one Android phone and one iPhone.

## Staff flow

- Open the staff link and sign in.
- Confirm the Home screen has no sideways scrolling at 360px, 390px, and 430px widths.
- Clock in, confirm the location message, then clock out.
- Start and end a break; confirm paid hours exclude the break.
- Open Rota, Timesheet, Activity, and Account.
- Submit a correction request and confirm it appears for the manager.
- Turn notifications on where the browser supports them.
- Install to the home screen, close the browser, and reopen the installed app.
- Turn on airplane mode and confirm the offline message is clear.

## Manager flow

- Open Now, Rota, Timesheets, Staff, Sites, Review, and Audit.
- Approve and reject a correction.
- Filter and export audit history.
- Export timesheets.
- Add or edit a worksite.
- Confirm the bottom navigation does not cover buttons or dialogs.

Record the phone model, browser, OS version, and any failed step before production release.
